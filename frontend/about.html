About The Platform

Learn the purpose, mission, and human-centered impact behind this community disaster management frontend.

Your Shield Against the Storm

This portal is designed to support disaster preparedness, incident reporting, relief visibility, and coordination across communities.

It combines citizen participation with authority workflows so local response becomes faster, clearer, and easier to manage. The multi-page structure now makes the frontend easier to maintain and expand.

Start Reporting

Mission

To improve emergency response through better reporting, verified coordination, and accessible community information.

Vision

A resilient digital platform where citizens and authorities collaborate before, during, and after disasters.
